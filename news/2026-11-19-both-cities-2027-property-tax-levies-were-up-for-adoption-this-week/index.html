Both cities' 2027 property tax levies were up for adoption this week

November 19, 2026 · Renton & Newcastle Dispatch, issue 1 · All Renton & Newcastle news

Renton's, held to the 1% state limit, costs the average homeowner about $5 more a year, the city says; its 2027–2028 budget had a first reading Monday. Newcastle's rate would go to $1.0763 per $1,000 of assessed value from $1.0303, using capacity banked in 2025 and 2026: on a home assessed at $1 million, about $46 more.
