This week in Renton & Newcastle.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 2 of the Renton & Newcastle Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • Northbound I-405 closes overnight between SR 167 and SR 169, Wednesday, December 2, 11:59 p.m. to 4 a.m. Thursday. Ramps start closing as early as 9 p.m., including the Talbot Road South (SR 515) on-ramp and the Exit 4 off-ramp to Bronson Way; a signed detour will be posted. WSDOT
  • Metro's survey on bus changes around downtown Renton closes Monday, November 30. More below. King County Metro
  • Logan Avenue South is closed to cars between the Transit Access Road and South 3rd Street through February 28, as a walkway between Piazza Park and Legacy Square for the market opening and holiday events. City of Renton, Nov. 23 agenda

What the city decided and what changed

The Renton History Museum lights its tree Saturday, and the city is lining up money for the building

The lighting runs 5 to 6:30 p.m.

Buses run a Sunday schedule today and a weekday schedule Friday

Renton's city facilities and King County offices are closed today and Friday, and so are the library branches in Renton, the Renton Highlands, Fairwood and Newcastle.

Metro is asking riders, through Monday, how buses should reach Renton's new transit center

The center is going up at South Grady Way and Rainier Avenue South and is set to open in 2028, when every stop at today's downtown transit center closes.
