Renton & Newcastle · Nov 19 to Nov 25

This week in Renton & Newcastle.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 1 of the Renton & Newcastle Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • Renton elementary schools let out early November 19, 20, 23 and 24 for conferences, and middle schools on the 23rd and 24th. Friday the 20th is not a late-start day. All schools let out early Wednesday, November 25; no school November 26 and 27. Renton School District
  • Issaquah School District schools, which serve part of Newcastle, are closed November 25 through 27. Issaquah School District

What the city decided and what changed

Renton is paying for a study of how to finish the lakefront trail from Gene Coulon Park to the Cedar River Trail

The Sam Chastain Trail would run along the south shore of Lake Washington to Cedar River Trail Park, a link first drawn in the city's 1990 trails plan.

Newcastle's council had a December 1 hearing on a transportation benefit district on Tuesday's agenda

A district would give the city its own money for street preservation and maintenance.

Both cities' 2027 property tax levies were up for adoption this week

Renton's, held to the 1% state limit, costs the average homeowner about $5 more a year, the city says; its 2027–2028 budget had a first reading Monday.

Renton's corner-store rule had its first reading Monday

It would allow cafés, bakeries, small groceries and barbers of up to 1,800 square feet on corner lots or within a half-mile walk of a major transit stop, open 7 a.m. to 9 p.m., with no drive-throughs.

leaves off the drain before the cold

Renton asks residents to clear leaves and debris from the storm drains on their street; a grate under wet leaves is how a rainy night puts water across the road.
