Why downtown Renton is so slow to change

November 26, 2026 · Renton & Newcastle Dispatch, issue 2 · Every answered question

A reader asked why downtown Renton is so slow to change. Part of the answer is what a downtown street project takes. The two-way conversion of Williams and Wells avenues South was awarded in March 2020, substantially finished in February 2022, physically complete in February 2024, settled with the contractor for $590,000 in January 2026, and formally accepted by the council on November 16. The rest is what is landing now: the city lists the $10.8 million Renton Market, the old Renton Pavilion rebuilt as a year-round enclosed market, as completed in 2026. Next comes the 2028 move of the transit center to Grady Way and Rainier Avenue; Metro says more than 56% of bus trips would still serve the downtown business district.
