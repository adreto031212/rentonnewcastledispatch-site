Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Renton & Newcastle newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Renton & Newcastle that is news too.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent Bellevue publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
